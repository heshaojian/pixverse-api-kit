Why REVOLVE

REVOLVE is a natural lighthouse account for AI product-video automation: a trend-led catalog, fast merchandising moments, creator-native customers, and constant need for fresh PDP, social, and paid creative variants. This pilot keeps the ask focused: ten SKUs, one polished demo per SKU, and product-accuracy scoring before a larger production run.

Pilot Plan

A tight pilot should prove whether Growth Studio can preserve product detail at the speed REVOLVE needs, then identify which creative formats are worth scaling.

01

SKU intake

REVOLVE shares a small set of product pages or campaign SKUs. Growth Studio converts each into a constrained video brief.

02

Generate and score

Create short vertical videos, then score natural motion, garment fidelity, merchandising fit, and channel readiness.

03

Scale winners

Expand the best patterns into PDP loops, creator-style variants, and paid-social hooks for seasonal drops.
