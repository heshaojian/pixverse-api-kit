# Plaud Public Video Reference Pitch Design

## Goal

Rebuild the Plaud pitch as a customer-facing decision page for Plaud marketers. The page should show how PixVerse can extend Plaud's existing public hardware and creator proof into three clearly differentiated video formats without presenting internal research or production mechanics.

## Audience And Decision

- Primary reader: Plaud product marketing, ecommerce, performance, and brand teams.
- Requested decision: approve a focused one-week hardware-video pilot.
- Primary action: plan the Plaud video pilot by email.

## Core Thesis

Plaud already has credible product, launch, guide, and creator proof. PixVerse can scale that foundation into product-page motion, performance and social ads, and premium launch films while keeping the device, recording action, workplace context, and trust cues visible.

## Page Story

1. A proof-first hero pairs the thesis with the strongest existing Plaud hardware demo.
2. A visible public video reference map translates Plaud source signals into three channel-specific PixVerse formats and shows one real concept video for each format.
3. A five-rule section distills the recurring public-video signals: hardware in context, obvious recording action, real workplaces, privacy awareness, and creator-led scale and use.
4. A compact hardware range keeps the pitch limited to Plaud Note Pro, Plaud Note, Plaud NotePin S, Plaud NotePin, and Plaud One.
5. A supporting proof shelf preserves the three existing product videos as concept examples, never as official Plaud work.
6. A focused pilot closes on one priority device, three distinct format masters, and one shared review.

## Visual Direction

- Use the PixVerse dark-only token system: black canvas, white-alpha depth, Plus Jakarta Sans, Inconsolata for utility labels, and the create gradient only for the final pilot action.
- Make the reference map the signature: each column follows the same compact sequence, `Plaud signal -> PixVerse format -> channel job`, so structure carries the strategy.
- Keep everything else restrained: precise rules, bounded portrait media, no decorative gradients, no card nesting, and no stock imagery.
- Desktop uses a three-column map and five-column hardware strip. Mobile becomes a single reading column with horizontally safe navigation and at least 44-pixel targets.

## Evidence And Copy Boundaries

- Link only to the public Plaud homepage, Plaud Note guide page, Plaud's official launch post, the SEA Plaud creator-proof section, the supplied independent NotePin S review, and canonical hardware product pages.
- Do not show prices, volatile ratings, software plans, Plaud Intelligence, Ask Plaud, templates, technical settings, model names, generation language, internal labels, or unsupported partnership language.
- Describe the six PixVerse MP4s as concept examples. Do not imply that they are official Plaud videos or customer-approved assets.
- Keep the noindex posture and the existing concept disclaimer.

## Verification

- Automated tests enforce section order, source links, exact hardware scope, the five public-video rules, three differentiated format cards, preserved media URLs, one primary action, deferred media, customer-safe language, design-system primitives, and valid mobile anchor offsets.
- Browser checks cover 1440 x 1000 desktop and 390 x 844 mobile, with no horizontal overflow, no hidden section anchors, usable controls, and bounded portrait media.
- Live availability checks cover every MP4, poster, product image, product link, and public reference link where practical.
